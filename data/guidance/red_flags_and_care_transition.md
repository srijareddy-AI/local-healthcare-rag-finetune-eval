# Red Flags and Care Transitions — Public Educational Summary

This is a short educational summary written for this demo project. It is NOT a
clinical guideline and NOT medical advice for any real patient.

A care transition is the move from hospital to home or to another care
setting. Transitions fail when information is lost: the follow-up appointment
is not booked, the medication list is inconsistent, or warning symptoms are
not explained. Common red-flag symptoms discussed in public patient education
include chest pain, worsening shortness of breath, fever, confusion, and rapid
swelling. A discharge plan should name a follow-up interval, a follow-up
clinic or clinician, and a contact route for the care team. If any of those
items is not in the discharge note, it is missing information — it should be
reported as missing, never invented.
